## 📐 Structural Hierarchy Breakdown

1. **Immediate Focus:** Bold statement headline.
2. **Compact Bio Card:** Photo is integrated inside a horizontal card alongside developer title & location. It doesn't dominate the page vertically.
3. **Skill Proof:** Immediate tech pill badges (`Next.js`, `TypeScript`, `Node.js`, `PostgreSQL`) right below the bio card.
4. **Balanced CTAs:** Side-by-side buttons for viewing work and contact.
