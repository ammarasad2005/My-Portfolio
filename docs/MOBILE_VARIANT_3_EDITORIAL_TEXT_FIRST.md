## 📐 Structural Hierarchy Breakdown

1. **Immediate Focus:** Clean, bold editorial typography describing developer discipline & location.
2. **Contextual Photo Placement:** Photo is converted into a small notebook paperclip annotation beside the "WHAT I DO" section header.
3. **Structured Service Cards:** 2x2 grid outlining core full stack capabilities.
4. **Project Focus:** Direct transition into project case study cards.
