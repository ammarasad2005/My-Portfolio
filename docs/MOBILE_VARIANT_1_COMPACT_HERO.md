## 📐 Structural Hierarchy Breakdown

1. **Immediate Focus (Above the Fold):** Headline + Short Subtitle + High-touch primary CTA button (`w-full py-3`).
2. **Personal Trust Anchor:** Compact horizontal developer identity bar showing photo avatar, name, location, and live availability badge.
3. **Value Proof:** Immediate 2x2 grid of services followed by full-width project showcase cards.
4. **Artistic Element:** Taped polaroid is downscaled to a subtle desk stamp at the bottom of the fold rather than occupying the prime top viewport.
